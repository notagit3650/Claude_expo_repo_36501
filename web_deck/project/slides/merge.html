All the new facts, nothing lost, no extra training. “Model soups”, Wortsman et al. 2022: average the weights.

Here's the trick. Take the three single-language models and literally average their weights: add the numbers, divide by three. No training. This is the "model soup" idea from Wortsman and colleagues, 2022. Result: as many new facts as the mixture, plus 5.1, and zero skill loss, it sits at the base model's level. It beats every single-language model on both axes. First time in this talk we get the facts without paying the price. And it means language-specific updates can be trained separately, even by different teams, and merged.

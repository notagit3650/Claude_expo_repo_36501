Here's the trick. While the model is answering, we simply add that arrow to its activity at one layer. One addition, no training, and nothing is forgotten because nothing was changed. The tip-of-the-tongue answer comes out. And where you add it matters: at the MLP layers, the model's memory blocks, factual recall improves by 45%, more than double the usual spot everyone steers. Remember that: facts live in the MLPs. But there's a hard limit. Steering can only surface what the model has already read. It can't tell it who won an election last year. For that, Way two.

So, what we achieved, back to the three reasons this matters. A local medical model: steer it, one nudge at the MLPs, no training, nothing forgotten. Genuinely new knowledge: hand it the newspaper, 2,500 times less text than from scratch, then average the language versions and the forgetting cancels out. Every language: train once, mostly, about 70% carries over, and test in every language so the hidden costs show. The thread through all of it: knowledge has an address and a direction. Next step: find the direction without training at all.

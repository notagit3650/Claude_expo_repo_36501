The cheaper way. Rebuilding a model from scratch, Llama 3 for example, means 15.6 trillion words and about 30 million GPU-hours. Instead we keep training the existing model, same recipe, on about six billion words of fresh news: 2,500 times less text, a few days. We built a clean archive of 205 million news articles for this, and it works, the model learns the news. It still forgets a little, so the question becomes: can we minimise that cost?

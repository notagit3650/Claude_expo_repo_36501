Old facts stay. General skills slip, mostly in the other languages. Can we take less of a hit, and transfer more?

Does it cost anything? Two kinds of old knowledge. Old facts, events before 2023: kept, even slightly up. But general skills, reading, science, commonsense, twenty tests in three languages: they slip. French-only loses 1.2 points, German 1.3, English 0.5. And it hides: test the French model only in French and it looks fine. This is catastrophic forgetting in a new disguise, and the multilingual lens is what makes it visible.

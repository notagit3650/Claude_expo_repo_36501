Training has a cost: what it used to know. Can we avoid training altogether?

The usual fix is to send it back to school: train it some more on medical text. Two problems. It's expensive, about 48 billion words, weeks on 8 GPUs. And it forgets: after medical training the model gets worse at simple things it could do before, like tracking who has the file. We call this catastrophic forgetting. So training has a cost beyond money: what the model used to know. Can we avoid training altogether?

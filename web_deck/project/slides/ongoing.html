What I'm working on now: where does this new knowledge live? Turns out, in the same place steering found. Keep only the changes in the MLP layers and about 95% of the new facts survive, and most of that sits in the first half of the layers. And the three language updates point in different directions; the average is the good direction. So here's the idea: if we know where it lives and which way it points, maybe we don't need to train three models and average them. Maybe we can probe the MLPs, find that direction, and nudge there directly, like steering, and skip most of the training.

Third reason this matters, and it's the one I worked on. In therapy, people open up in their own language: Dewaele and Costa followed 182 multilingual clients and found they switch to their first language when the emotional tone rises. So our models have to work in German, in French, not just English. Do we have to train once per language? That would multiply the cost. If what it learns in one language carries over to the others, we train once. Does it?
